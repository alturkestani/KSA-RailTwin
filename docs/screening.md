Label: **screening input, not a forecast**. Scores rank candidates for further study; they are not demand, cost or benefit estimates. ## 1. Method and chain

1. Merge of the 13 region registers (`screening.merge`, 1,678 rows, 0 duplicates; `runs/r2/merge`).
2. Primary-city matching (`screening.primary_match`, A-r2-001…003; §2). INTEGRATE_R2 added the national citypopulation.de Census 2022 relay page (S-qassim-003; region from its region code) and the OSM place polygons (`osm-gcc-place-areas-extract-2026-10-14`) to the relay-bridge tier, and new judgement decisions (CP3 4a).
3. Control-total scaling of grid rows (`screening.control_total`, A-screen-040; §3); grid rows with a raw WorldPop value of 0 leave the register (CP3 5a; kept in the settlement universe).
4. Long list (`screening.funnel long-list`, filters below), short list (`score`, ±25 % one-at-a-time sweep, A-screen-029), sensitivities (5k threshold, linear rail gap, committed layer, grid ÷ 0.71).
5. Corridor families (`screening.corridors`, `docs/candidates.md` §4) and the coverage audit (`docs/coverage.md`).
