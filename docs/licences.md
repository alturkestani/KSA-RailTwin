# Licence & openness register

Rule 12 of `PROJECT.md`: every dataset, model, tool, voice, font, music track and image appears here with its licence, whether it is open, whether commercial use is allowed, the attribution text, share-alike or other obligations, and where it is used. `docs/licences.csv` is the machine-readable copy (same rows, same order); the `licence-register` skill says how to add a row and how to run the release-compatibility check. **Status** says how the licence was established: `verified <date>` (read at the source), `verified (TrafficTwin)` (established in the Jeddah project's registers), `unverified` (from memory or secondary; must be verified before the item is used), `planned` (not yet used), `per file/track at use`. **Approval** is required for any proprietary or non-open item (rule 12) and names Tariq and the date.

Open-source-first decision rule: an open alternative that does the job wins; a non-open item is used only when no open alternative exists, is flagged here, explained, and approved by Tariq before use. Known traps: ODbL share-alike on derived databases (OSM), non-commercial model licences (Coqui XTTS-v2 CPML, MMS-TTS CC-BY-NC, F5-TTS and Fish-Speech weights), CC-BY-NC/ND music, the Remotion company licence, LLM community licences (Llama) and Arabic model terms (Jais, ALLaM) that must be read on the exact model card.
