# Cost register v1: KSA data points, bottom-up library, reconciliation and reference-class uplifts (W3)

**Cost ranges, price year 2025, not a budget.** Money is in SAR million at 2025 prices unless a table says "as published". Every figure traces to a register id (`cities/ksa/costs/cost_register.yaml`), an uplift id (`cities/ksa/costs/uplifts.yaml`), a source id (`docs/sources/cost-*.csv`, `docs/sources/index.csv`) or a run under `runs/costs/`. Modelled outputs carry the label **"synthetic demand, uncalibrated: scenario comparison, not a forecast"**: the fixture and reference corridors below are test shapes, not proposed lines, and their costs are ranges (P10/P50/P90; uplifted P50/P80), never points. Text marked **analysis** is our reading, not a sourced fact.

## 2. Price base, index and conventions

- **One index (CP2 P2a, Tariq, 31 Oct 2026):** `cities/ksa/costs/index.yaml` is the only price index; the R1b benchmark index (`cities/ksa/benchmarks/index-r1b.yaml`, A-bench-001, superseded) was folded in, keeping its source rows S-bench-idx-001…003 (WDI deflators and exchange rates 1985–1999, Portugal, EUR per USD 1999), and deleted. Existing factor values for the register did not change (all 882 pre-P2a factor ids rebuilt with the same value; 392 `-cti` rows now cite only the series they use). Per-economy EUR rows (`I-eur-fra-…`, `country:` FRA/DEU/ESP/ITA/PRT) serve the benchmark rows; the register uses the euro-area row. Benchmark before/after: `docs/benchmarks/normalisation-delta.md`.
- **Base:** 2025 SAR (A-cost-001, approved 30 Oct 2026). **Central method** (A-cost-002, approved at CP2 P2a): SAR items are inflated with the Saudi CPI (S-cost-idx-001, the WDI relay of the GASTAT CPI); foreign-currency items are inflated with the source economy's GDP deflator (WDI, S-cost-idx-002 and S-bench-idx-001) before conversion, converted at the 2025 average rate (S-cost-idx-003) to USD, then to SAR at the peg 3.75 (S-lb-020). Series exceptions (A-cost-307): EUR without a euro-area economy uses the Eurostat EA20 deflator (S-cost-idx-004; WDI has no euro-area deflator); AED uses the UAE CPI (S-cost-idx-001; WDI has no 2025 UAE deflator). **Sensitivity:** convert at the source year's rate, then inflate with the Saudi CPI (factor ids ending `-cti`). The choice matters: EUR 2016 → SAR 2025 is 5.41 (central, `I-eur-2016-sar-2025`) against 4.78 (sensitivity, `I-eur-2016-sar-2025-cti`).
- **Figures priced in 2026 or 2027** (2026 awards; the 2027 tariff) are treated as 2025 prices because no annual 2026 index is published yet (A-cost-009; Saudi CPI rose 2.0 % from 2024 to 2025, S-cost-idx-001, so the bias is of that order). Factor ids end `-asbase`.
- The Saudi GDP deflator fell from 2024 to 2025 (oil prices; S-cost-idx-002), so it is not used for domestic costs; the CPI is (A-cost-001). The GASTAT CPI primary table is still to fetch: WDI relays it.
- **Units.** The `SAR` token in a unit names the base currency; until normalisation, values are millions (or units) of the row's `currency` at its `price_year`. Non-money units (`pct`, `pct_of_capex`, `pct_per_yr`, `years`, `kwh_per_train_km`, `fte_per_km`) are never converted.
- **Single published figures.** Many contract and award rows have low = central = high because the source prints one number (each row says so in `notes`). They are not modelled points: in an estimate they are spread by the other items and by the reference-class uplift, and conflicting figures for the same thing are separate rows, never averaged.
- **Statuses:** `sourced` = as printed; `derived` = our arithmetic on sourced figures (the `derivation` field shows it); `assumed` = no source found anywhere searched, with a range and an `A-cost` id; `unknown` = item named, values empty, `searched` says where.

### 4.1 What the KSA points say (analysis)

- **Haramain HSR (conflict C-004: resolved with evidence).** The "USD 9.4 bn" and "6,736 million" figures both describe the Phase 2 (Al Shoula consortium: systems, rolling stock and O&M; S-008) contract, EUR 6.736 bn (C-system-hhr-phase2-eur-001; S-cost-ksa-004, S-cost-ksa-005), also stated as SR 30.8 bn at signing (C-system-hhr-phase2-sar-001). The programme figures are SR 51.5 bn (Dec 2012 estimate, after both main contracts were awarded) and SR 60 bn (Minister, Mar 2018) or SR 63 bn including expropriation (2015) (C-programme-hhr-outturn-001). The all-in top-down per route-km is SAR 150–159 m (2025 prices; C-programme-hhr-per-km-001), which bundles stations of Haramain class, trains and 12 years of O&M. A "USD 7.87 bn" figure (2018, S-cost-ksa-011) is below the Phase 2 contract alone and is kept as an outlier row, "do not use".
- **Haramain ridership vs forecast.** The only forecast found is "> 3 m passengers a year" (2012); 60 m a year is a design capacity, not a forecast. The 2023 outturn is 6.97 m (S-012, secondary), about 2.3× the early forecast and about 12 % of capacity (analysis; logs of the KSA pass). No forecast with a horizon year was found, so Haramain cannot test the ridership reference class (§7). Ridership is not a register (cost) row.
- **Riyadh Metro (C-005: resolved as two vintages).** USD 22.5 bn = the three July 2013 design-build packages (USD 9.45 + 5.21 + 7.82 = 22.48 bn; C-programme-riyadh-metro-award-2013-001); USD 25 bn = the 2024 outturn stated by the RCRC CEO (C-programme-riyadh-metro-outturn-001; S-038, ENR snippet S-cost-ksa-036). The ENR "USD 25 B" headline is therefore consistent with the RCRC CEO's figure, but ENR itself was only seen as a search snippet (HTTP 403). Still open: the stated USD 166 m/km implies 150.6 km, not the 176 km network. In real 2025 terms the 2013 award sum (SAR 114.7 bn) exceeds the 2024 outturn (SAR 96.4 bn) because the award is inflated over 12 years with the US deflator while the outturn is a sum of nominal payments over 2013–2024 (analysis): neither is a real-terms outturn, which is why the RCF check (§7) uses schedule only.

Per km: SAR 9.2 m (2012 basis) or SAR 4.1–4.2 m (2017 basis), single-track and freight-led.
- **SAR East.** Only phases are priced: the Hofuf–Haradh doubling (SAR 1.9 m/km), a 78 km renewal (SAR 1.9 m/km), the Hofuf bypass for 200 km/h (lump), the Dammam Second Industrial City link (lump), Talgo (2015) and Stadler (2024 base and 2026 option) trainsets; the Stadler figures bundle 10 years of maintenance.

## 6. Estimator method and the fixture result

Method (`costs/estimate.py`): each component's quantity comes from the corridor (length, stations by class, electrification) and the profile (terrain and structure shares, signalling level, depots, substation spacing); its unit cost is a PERT draw on the register row's low/central/high (A-cost-004). All capex items share one uniform draw, opex items another and asset lives a third (comonotonic, A-cost-003), so ranges do not shrink by diversification. Land, utilities and design/PM are percentages of construction; contingency is reported but not added (A-cost-008). Fleet = ceil(cycle time / headway) per service, plus a 10 % spare (A-cost-005, -006), with the running time from the corridor's acceleration–cruise–braking and dwell parameters; train-km from headway × span × 365 days (A-cost-007). Opex per year = driver × product of register rows (for example HSR energy = kWh per train-km × tariff × train-km). Renewals: component capex × renewal shares, once per asset life inside 30/40/60 years (undiscounted, real; A-cost-016). Reference-class uplift: base capex draws × capex multiplier draws (`costs.rcf`), stage `strategic` (A-rcf-032). Every component writes its register ids to `trace.csv`; `meta.json` lists the seed, config hash, manifest hash, code version, register ids and assumption ids.
