**Synthetic demand, uncalibrated: scenario comparison, not a forecast.** These items are **not monetised and never added to any BCR** (`economics.benefits.MATRIX`: `regional_development_vision2030` and `tourism_hajj_umrah_capacity` are `scorecard` streams). No source was found that monetises regional development, Vision 2030 alignment or Hajj/Umrah capacity for rail in KSA (rule 1), so the scorecard records facts from the registers only:

- regions served: the regions of the short-list towns that generated the corridor (`generated_from_ids` in the corridor YAML; `cities/ksa/candidates.parquet`);
- economic roles at those towns, as scored in the register (R2/R4 roles; low-confidence oasis roles stay unscored, CP5 H6a);
- whether a Hajj/Umrah node or a giga-project, tourism or heritage role is served;
- whether a served region's Saudi unemployment rate is above the Kingdom's 7.2 % (GASTAT LFS Q4 2025, S-soc-015/016; A-cba-311), a labour-market indicator of regional need, not a measure of benefit.

Vision 2030 programme documents name rail and logistics targets (for example the National Transport and Logistics Strategy); R5 does not score alignment with them beyond these facts, because a score would be a judgement without a sourced method. The project stays non-partisan (rule 9): the scorecard describes, it does not advocate.
