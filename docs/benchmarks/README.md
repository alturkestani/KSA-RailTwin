## Cost per route-km (as published, and converted to SAR 2025)

Conversion with the project's only price index, `cities/ksa/costs/index.yaml` (CP2 P2a; A-cost-002). Central: inflate with the source economy's GDP deflator (World Bank WDI) to 2025 before conversion, convert at the 2025 rate to USD, then SAR at the peg 3.75 (EUR figures in a euro-area economy use that economy's deflator; series exceptions A-cost-307: EUR elsewhere uses the Eurostat EA20 deflator, AED the UAE CPI). Sensitivity (`-cti` factors): convert at the price-year rate and the peg, then inflate with the Saudi CPI. The before/after change against the former R1b routes A/B is logged in `normalisation-delta.md`. Cross-country cost comparisons are indicative: scope (rolling stock, land, stations, financing), terrain and tunnel share differ by line and are given in the scope column and the dossier. Level (from `cities/ksa/benchmarks/cost_levels.yaml`): `line` = a whole line or section, `average` = a network or speed-class average, `estimate` = the estimate at decision for a line now built, `package` = one contract or project lot, `unbuilt` = a line not yet in service, `other` = a cross-country average or model input.
