## 4. What is blocked or unresolved

- **GASTAT primary tables are still unreachable.** On 31 Oct 2026 `portal.saudicensus.sa` gave no connection, stats.gov.sa had no locality file in the pages read, and open.data.gov.sa's API rejected the query (S-cp1-026…028). Every population is a secondary relay, and the governorate count rests on S-073.
- **Riyadh seats:** 14 of 14 now have a population, but all are secondary. 13 seat identities are inferred from name matches and Layla comes from the pilot register. Al-Khamasin (named as the Wadi ad-Dawasir seat) is unresolved (S-cp1-025).
- **Al-Qarā:** located at the OSM admin centre node 9327998071 (S-cp1-003). It has no settlement population (not tabulated, S-cp1-005). Wikidata and Wikipedia place it about 1,077 km away in Al-Jawf; that is a conflict and was not used (S-cp1-006, S-cp1-007).
- **Unrecoverable scripts:** none of the named files. One-off shell commands typed in the pilots exist only in the gitignored logs (`screening/pilot_builders/README.md`). The 40 Qassim Nominatim JSONs are still not in the manifest.
- **citypopulation.de:** the figures may be reused, including commercially, with attribution. The licence version conflicts: CC BY 3.0 in the terms vs CC BY 4.0 in the page HTML (S-cp1-050, S-cp1-055). Whole pages and archived HTML copies are not licensed for redistribution (S-cp1-050).
- **Appraisal sources not reached:** the 2022 Green Book PDF (S-cp1-064, unverified) and the EC's own host of the 2014 guide (read from a JASPERS/EIB copy, S-cp1-067).
- **Cost-log outcomes:** 4 pilot runs are `blocked` because their status files say so: al_baha, madinah, najran, qassim. PILOT_MERGE later normalised their registers, but the run outcome stays as recorded. CP1_PREP asked for `done` on all 13; the status files do not support that.
- **Rail gap after the cap:** the criterion has almost no rank correlation with the total score (Spearman −0.01) because most rows sit near the cap (`docs/screening-pilot.md` §6).
