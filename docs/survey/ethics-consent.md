# Intercity travel survey: ethics, consent and data handling

**Designed, not fielded.** No respondent has been contacted, no form has been created, no data have been collected (PROJECT.md rule 7). This note is a draft for a future human fielding institution, which becomes the data controller, fills every placeholder in square brackets, and obtains ethics approval before any interview (`docs/survey/design.md`, section 9). It is not legal advice; points that could not be verified are written "unclear: <question>".

The study is personal, independent and non-partisan: it does not promote any operator, line, agency, company or policy.

## 1. Consent text (English)

*Shown in full before any question after the screening section. Placeholders in square brackets are filled by the fielding institution; the text may not be shortened without the ethics committee's agreement.*

> **Study on travel between cities in Saudi Arabia**
>
> **Who is asking.** This survey is carried out by **[name of the fielding institution]**, which is responsible for your answers. It is part of an independent research study of how people travel between cities in Saudi Arabia. The study does not represent any transport operator, company or government body, and it does not promote any project.
>
> **Why.** We want to understand how people choose between car, train, plane and coach for trips between cities, and which features of a journey matter to them. The answers are used only for this research, and only in aggregate (as totals and averages).
>
> **What you will do.** You will answer questions about your recent trips, choose between travel options on about ten screens, and answer some questions about yourself. It takes about **[20]** minutes.
>
> **Taking part is voluntary.** You can skip any question you do not want to answer, except the questions needed to show you the right screens (your age group, region and recent trip). You can stop at any time without giving a reason, and nothing you have answered will be kept if you stop before the end. Not taking part has no consequence for you. **[If an incentive is offered: describe it here, and state that it does not depend on any particular answer.]**
>
> **What we do not ask.** We do not ask your name, ID or Iqama number, phone number, email or address. We do not record your IP address or device identifiers. We do not ask about your religious beliefs, health, tribe or political views.
>
> **How your answers are protected.** Your answers are stored on secure systems inside Saudi Arabia and are not transferred outside the Kingdom. Only the named research team can access them. Results are published only as totals and averages, and no table will show a group so small that a person could be recognised.
>
> **How long we keep them.** The answer file is kept for up to **[24]** months after the survey closes, so that the analysis can be checked, and is then destroyed. After that, only a version from which nothing can identify anyone is kept.
>
> **Your rights.** Under the Personal Data Protection Law you have the right to know why your data are collected, to access them, to obtain a copy, to ask for correction, and to ask for their destruction, and you may withdraw your consent at any time. Because we do not collect your name or contact details, we can only find your answers through the **random code** shown on the last screen. Keep it if you may want to ask us to delete your answers; requests are accepted for **[30]** days after you finish.
>
> **Questions or concerns.** Contact: **[to be filled by the fielding institution]** (data protection contact: **[to be filled by the fielding institution]**). This study was reviewed by **[name of the research ethics committee, approval number and date: to be filled by the fielding institution]**.
>
> By choosing "Yes, I agree" you confirm that you are 18 or over, that you have read this information, and that you agree to take part and to the use of your answers as described.

## 3. Legal frame

### 3.1 Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL)

Issued by Royal Decree M/19 of 9/2/1443H and amended by Royal Decree M/148 of 5/9/1444H; text read on the Bureau of Experts portal (S-soc-323, S-soc-324). The articles below were read in the official Arabic text, including the amendment notes; the Implementing Regulation could not be read (S-soc-361), so anything that the law delegates to the regulations is marked unclear in section 5.

| article | what it says (summary; source) | how this design responds |
|---|---|---|
| Art. 1 (definitions) | Personal data include any data that make identification possible directly or indirectly; the controller is the body that determines the purpose and manner of processing (S-soc-355) | The fielding institution is the controller. The response file (governorate, sex, age band, nationality, household size, income band) could make a person identifiable indirectly in a small governorate, so it is **treated as personal data** at collection (A-social-138) |
| Art. 1(11) as amended (sensitive data) | Includes religious belief, health and genetic data, among others (S-soc-340) | No question on belief, health, tribe or politics (design.md, section 8). Whether the prayer-facility attribute and item E3 reveal religious belief is unclear (section 5, Q2) |
| Art. 2 (scope) | Applies to processing of personal data in the Kingdom by any means (S-soc-356) | The survey falls in scope |
| Art. 4 as amended (rights) | Rights to be informed of the legal basis and purpose, to access, to obtain a readable copy, to correction and to destruction of data no longer needed (S-soc-328) | Stated in the consent text; exercised through the random completion code (section 4.5) |
| Art. 5 as amended (consent) | No processing without the subject's consent except as the law provides; the regulations set when consent must be **explicit** ("صريحة" replaced "كتابية"); consent may be withdrawn at any time (S-soc-324) | Explicit opt-in at Q-B1, before any substantive question; withdrawal at any time (section 4.5) |
| Art. 7 | Consent may not be a condition for a service or benefit unrelated to the processing (S-soc-345) | Any incentive is described in the consent text and does not depend on answers; see section 5, Q4 |
| Art. 8 as amended | The controller chooses a processor that provides the guarantees needed and verifies its compliance (S-soc-362) | Panel or platform providers contracted as processors, inside the Kingdom |
| Art. 10 as amended | Data collected from the subject directly and processed only for the purpose collected; exception where data are not kept in identifiable form (para. 6) (S-soc-357) | Data collected only from the respondent; used only for this research purpose; no linkage to other data |
| Art. 11 | Purpose directly related to the controller's purposes; content limited to the minimum needed; stop collecting and destroy data no longer needed (S-soc-341) | Data minimisation (section 4.2) |
| Art. 12 as amended | A privacy policy available when data are collected, covering purpose, content, method, storage, processing, destruction and rights (S-soc-342) | The fielding institution adopts and links its privacy policy from the consent screen |
| Art. 13 as amended | When collecting directly, inform the subject of the legal basis, purpose, mandatory or optional items, collector identity, recipients and any transfer abroad, consequences of not completing, and the Art. 4 rights (S-soc-343) | Each element appears in the consent text; the legal basis (consent) is stated by the choice "Yes, I agree"; see section 5, Q1 on wording the legal basis |
| Art. 18 as amended (destruction) | Destroy personal data after the purpose ends, without delay; may keep them after that if everything identifying the subject is removed (S-soc-327) | Retention rule (section 4.4, A-social-139) |
| Arts. 19–20 | Organisational, administrative and technical protection measures, including in transfer; notify the competent authority of a leak, and the subject where it may harm them (S-soc-344) | Storage and breach handling (section 4.3) |
| Art. 27 (research) | Data may be collected or processed for scientific, research or statistical purposes **without consent** if they do not identify the subject specifically, or if identifiers are destroyed during processing and the data are not sensitive, or another law or prior agreement requires it (S-soc-325) | The design **does not rely on** this exemption: consent is taken anyway, because the research-ethics law requires informed consent for research on humans (S-soc-347) and the indirect-identification test is uncertain (A-social-137) |
| Art. 28 | Official identity documents may not be photographed or copied except under a law or at an authority's request (S-soc-358) | No identity document is requested, seen or copied |
| Art. 29 as amended (outside the Kingdom) | Transfer or disclosure abroad only for listed purposes and subject to conditions (no harm to national security or vital interests; adequate protection per the competent authority's assessment; minimum data), details in the regulations (S-soc-326) | **No transfer abroad**: collection platform, storage and analysis are inside the Kingdom (section 4.3). An online-survey platform hosted abroad would need an Art. 29 assessment first (section 5, Q3) |
| Art. 30(2) as amended | The regulations set when a controller must appoint a personal data protection officer (S-soc-359) | Contact placeholder for a data protection contact in the consent text; whether one is mandatory is unclear (section 5, Q5) |
| Art. 43 as amended | The law takes effect 720 days after publication (S-soc-329) | The law is treated as in force for any fielding |

### 3.2 Law of Ethics of Research on Living Creatures

Issued by Royal Decree M/59 of 14/9/1431H (S-soc-330); age definitions in Art. 1 repealed by Royal Decree M/191 of 29/11/1444H (S-soc-331). Implementing Regulation no. 4746 of 25/9/1443H known only from an aggregator summary, text not read (S-soc-332, secondary).

| article | what it says (summary; source) | how this design responds |
|---|---|---|
| Art. 1 | "Research" is a systematic experimental inquiry aimed at advancing the life sciences or enriching or developing general knowledge, using a living creature or parts of it; "living creature" covers humans (S-soc-330) | Whether a non-interventional travel questionnaire is "research" in this sense is **unclear** (section 5, Q6). The design treats it as in scope (A-social-142) |
| Art. 3 | An establishment may not allow research until the procedures under the law are completed (S-soc-360) | No fielding before ethics approval (design.md, section 9, item 3) |
| Art. 10 | Each establishment forms a local committee of at least five members, which checks conformity with Saudi regulations, checks informed-consent procedures and approves the research ethically (S-soc-346) | Approval by the fielding institution's registered local committee, cited in the consent text |
| Arts. 11–13 | No research on a person before informed consent; the researcher explains all possible consequences understandably, including of withdrawing; consent documented per the regulation (S-soc-347) | Consent text (sections 1–2); consent is recorded as the Q-B1 answer with a timestamp, without identity (section 4.5). Whether an electronic tick satisfies "documented" is unclear (section 5, Q7) |
| Art. 14 | The local committee may approve research without informed consent where information cannot be linked to the person (S-soc-348) | Not relied on: consent is taken |

Practice example: at least one Saudi university runs a sub-committee that reviews humanities and social research collecting non-medical data on humans (S-soc-333, secondary search summary). This suggests, but does not prove, that local committees review studies like this one.

**Age.** The study admits adults of 18 or over only (A-social-121). This is a rule of the study, not a reading of the law: the law's own age definitions were repealed (S-soc-331), and the conditions for consent by a guardian are set by the PDPL regulations, which were not read (S-soc-361).

## 4. Data handling

### 4.1 Anonymity

- No name, national ID or Iqama number, phone number, email, exact address or GPS location is asked or stored (design.md, section 8).
- No IP address, device identifier, cookie identifier or panel member ID is stored with the answers. If an online panel is used, the panel provider keeps its member list separately and passes the survey only a one-time token; the token is discarded at submission and the panel receives only "completed / not completed" (A-social-143).
- Paradata recorded automatically are limited to: interview language, assigned block, season framing, task order, completion time and the Q-B1 consent timestamp (instrument, Section G).
- The random completion code (section 4.5) is generated at submission, is not derived from any respondent data, and is shown once to the respondent.

### 4.2 Data minimisation (PDPL Art. 11, S-soc-341)

Every question has a stated analysis use; a question without one is removed before fielding.

| block | items | use |
|---|---|---|
| Screening | A1–A6 | eligibility, region and governorate (for stratification and the trip pivot), urban/non-urban stratum |
| Revealed trips | C1–C4 | trip frequency and mode by purpose; pivot distance D; availability of alternatives in estimation |
| Choice tasks | D1–D12 | mode-choice estimation; attribute non-attendance |
| Attitudes | E1–E10 | hypothesis tests H1, H2, H4 (design.md, section 2); attention check |
| About you | F1–F12 | segmentation and weighting to official margins; income is optional |

Governorate (A4) is kept in the raw file only for the pivot and for weighting; the analysis file keeps region and the urban/non-urban stratum, and governorate is dropped before any file leaves the controller's secure environment (A-social-143).

### 4.3 Storage, security and location

- Collection platform, storage and analysis environment **inside Saudi Arabia**, operated or contracted by the controller; no copy outside the Kingdom (PDPL Art. 29, S-soc-326). Any processor (panel or platform provider) is chosen for the guarantees it provides and checked by the controller (PDPL Art. 8, S-soc-362); the detailed processor rules are in the regulations (Q3).
- Encryption at rest and in transit; access limited to the named analysts listed in the ethics application; an access log kept (PDPL Art. 19, S-soc-344).
- A breach is notified to the competent authority, and to respondents where it may harm them, as Art. 20 requires (S-soc-344); since respondents are not identifiable, notice to them would be by public statement on the controller's site.
- This repository never holds the raw response file. Only the de-identified, aggregated estimation outputs (coefficients, covariance matrices, weighted tables with small cells suppressed) may enter the repository, and only after the verifier checks them (rule 8: nothing under `data/` or `runs/` is committed).

### 4.4 Retention and destruction (PDPL Art. 18, S-soc-327)

- Raw response file (including governorate and paradata): kept for at most 24 months after the survey closes, for re-estimation and audit, then destroyed (A-social-139).
- De-identified analysis file (region, stratum, segments, choices; no governorate, no paradata, no codes): may be kept after that, as Art. 18(1) allows once everything identifying the subject is removed.
- Incomplete interviews: answers of respondents who stop before the end are discarded at the end of each fieldwork day (A-social-141).
- Published outputs: no table cell, segment estimate or map shows fewer than 10 respondents (A-social-144).

### 4.5 Consent, withdrawal and rights

- **Consent.** Explicit opt-in at Q-B1, after the full consent text and before any substantive question (PDPL Art. 5, S-soc-324; Research Ethics Law Arts. 11–13, S-soc-347). A "No" ends the interview and nothing is stored except a count of refusals.
- **Withdrawal during the interview.** The respondent may stop at any time; partial answers are discarded (A-social-141).
- **Withdrawal after submission.** The closing screen shows a random completion code; for 30 days after completion (A-social-140) the respondent can ask the controller to delete the answers attached to that code. After that window, and in any case once the raw file is destroyed, the answers can no longer be linked to anyone.
- **Access, copy and correction (PDPL Art. 4, S-soc-328).** Available through the same code within the same window. Because the controller holds no identity, it cannot verify that the requester is the respondent; possession of the code is the only test (see Q8).

### 4.6 Respectful design (rule 9)

- Items E1–E10 test hypotheses; they make no claim about Saudi travellers, and the reports will not present them as cultural facts.
- The women-only or family carriage and prayer-area attributes describe features of a service. They are not questions about the respondent's beliefs or practices.
- The study is independent: the consent text names no operator, line or project, and no logo or sponsor appears.
