## 3. Failures (exact evidence)

**F1 (A1, A10). One corridor's formation profile violates its grade cap and the violation is not reported.**
- `runs/align/corridors-s1/S4-commuter_belt-abha-khamis-mushait-315/summary.json`: `"max_grade_cap": 0.025`, `"formation_max_grade": 12.68633`, `"formation_cap_ok": false`, `"ground_share_over_cap": 0.57`, leg 2 `"ends_relaxed": true` (An-Nimas → Al-Majaridah, straight 22.1 km, routed 27.2 km).
- `profile.parquet` rows 1247–1249: leg 1 ends at s = 124.705 km, ground 2,354.80 m, formation 2,354.80 m; leg 2 starts at s = 124.804 km, ground 2,354.99 m, formation 1,087.44 m (structure "tunnel"), then reaches ground at ~134 km and runs as "viaduct" to the end (462.5 m ground, 554.3 m formation at 151.9 km).
- `network/align.py:703–713`: when the LP is infeasible the station bounds are freed and `relaxed = True`; nothing downstream (route-all, apply, evaluate, the doc tables, the status block) surfaces `formation_cap_ok == false`. `docs/alignments.md:43–44` describes `ends_relaxed` and the re-check but does not say any corridor fails it.
- Consequence: the corridor's structure km (29.9 tunnel / 28.7 viaduct), its capex (`docs/alignments-tables.md` §4: 11.40 → 24.99 bn P50) and its rank changes (+19 / +9) rest on a physically impossible profile (a 1,267 m step at a station). At the 2.5 % commuter cap, 1,893 m of descent needs at least 75.7 km of line; the leg has 27.2 km, so the honest result is "cap cannot be met on this leg at any structure mix; corridor needs a longer alignment, a different class rule, or exclusion", stated in the status block and the tables.
- All 66 other corridors, the 4 alternatives and the 3 validation lines pass the cap check (verifier recomputation within legs and the summaries' own flag).

**F2 (A9, minor). One untraced figure.** `docs/alignments.md` §2: "OSM natural=sand/dune (about 834,000 km² mapped in KSA)". No run output, log, register row or source gives this number.
