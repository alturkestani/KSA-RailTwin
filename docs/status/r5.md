### Decisions taken in R5 (no one to ask; recorded)
1. User benefits = rule of a half on the logsum composite cost (car IVT minutes) valued at the appraisal VoT; VoT central = PPP income transfer (A-cba-106, **pending Tariq**).
2. Reliability not monetised in the central case (no KSA variability data); freight not monetised (no open freight OD); international visitors carry no user benefits; Jeddah decongestion zero (no candidate representable in TrafficTwin; R4 run ids recorded).
3. Ridership reference-class ratio (Flyvbjerg rail, median ≈ 0.69) applied to every draw, shared with costs.rcf.
4. WEI effective density on the logsum composite with baseline trip weights and a 15-minute own-zone cap (A-cba-307); TAG's average-GC definition gave a falling density in a development test, hence the method warning.
5. S5 capex = station item(s) only; station opex 2 % of capex a year (A-cba-314, assumed).
6. Construction starts 2027 for every case, PV year 2026 (A-cba-301).
