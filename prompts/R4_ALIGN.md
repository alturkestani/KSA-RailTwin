# R4_ALIGN — terrain-aware alignments for every candidate corridor (worktree `r4`, after `R4`, before `INTEGRATE_R4`; claude-opus-5-5)

Tariq's addition to R4 (1 Nov 2026, 01:00): "before running times, isochrones or corridor costs: terrain-aware alignments for every candidate corridor, replacing the straight sketches." R4 had already started when this arrived, so this label runs in the `r4` worktree after `R4: done` and **re-runs everything downstream that R4 computed on straight sketches** (running times, isochrones and the 30-minute test, corridor costs, screening and corridor generation). Use `gis-network-engineer` for the routing, `cost-estimator` for the tunnel/viaduct costing hooks, `screening-analyst` for the re-run; foreground subagents only. Register every parameter (`A-align-0xx`), every dataset (manifest + licence register: Copernicus DEM already in; WDPA, OSM landuse, the landcover/geology source, any restricted-zone source) and every number (`S-r4a-`). Status label `R4_ALIGN`.

## Tariq's specification (verbatim)
1. Method: least-cost path routing on Copernicus DEM 30 m (already in use), on a cost surface built from slope, with hard caps by line class (HSR ≤ 2.5%, mixed passenger/freight ≤ 1.25%; justify any change). Add minimum curve radius by design speed (e.g. HSR 300 km/h ≥ 7,000 m), and costs for tunnels and viaducts where the gradient cap can't be met at grade.
2. Avoid or penalise: urban cores (OSM landuse), protected areas (WDPA), sabkha and dune fields (open geology/landcover), wadis (prefer crossing, not running along the flood path), and military or restricted zones where openly known. Prefer existing transport corridors (highways, existing rail) where they help.
3. Validate the method against reality first: route HHR Makkah–Madinah, SAR North and SAR East with the same algorithm and compare with their OSM alignments. Report length error, Hausdorff/mean offset, and share of tunnel or viaduct. Target: length within ±5% and mean offset < 2 km. Tune the cost weights on these, then freeze them before routing the candidates.
4. Outputs per corridor: alignment GeoJSON, length vs straight line (detour ratio), gradient and elevation profile, tunnel/viaduct/earthworks km, and the max design speed achievable by segment. These feed the running-time model and the P10/P50/P90 costs.
5. Re-run screening and corridor generation on the routed lengths and times, and note any town whose rank changes by more than 5 places.
6. Maps: routed alignments over a hillshade, with straight sketches shown faintly for comparison.

## Coordinator framing (binding)
- **Order:** (a) data and licence rows (WDPA via the Protected Planet download terms — read them; if non-commercial, flag and use the OSM `boundary=protected_area` layer as the open fallback; OSM landuse; a landcover source for sabkha/dunes — ESA WorldCover 2021 (CC BY 4.0) or Copernicus Global Land Cover; wadis from OSM `waterway=*` + DEM flow accumulation; restricted zones only from openly published sources, else "not available" in the register); (b) cost surface and router (`network/align.py`: raster least-cost path on the DEM window per corridor, 8- or 16-connected, slope cost with the class cap as a hard mask, curvature handled by post-smoothing to the minimum radius and re-checking the cap; tunnel/viaduct segments inserted where the cap cannot be met at grade, with the thresholds registered); (c) **validation on HHR, SAR North, SAR East** against `cities/ksa/lines/*` OSM alignments — length error, Hausdorff and mean offset, tunnel/viaduct share — tune, then **freeze the weights in `cities/ksa/assumptions/align_weights.yaml` with a hash recorded in every corridor's `meta.json`**; (d) route the 76 corridors (S2–S5; every candidate, including the dominated ones' alternatives if cheap) and write the per-corridor outputs; (e) re-run running times, isochrones/30-minute test, corridor P10/P50/P90 costs (terrain and structure shares now measured, not proxied), screening and corridor generation; (f) maps.

If routing all 76 at 30 m is too slow on this Mac, route at 90 m for screening and at 30 m for the short-list corridors, and say so (A-align-0xx).
- **Report in the status block:** validation table (3 lines × metrics, target met Y/N), frozen weights hash, 76 corridors routed (detour ratio distribution; tunnel/viaduct km totals), rank changes > 5 places with names, the effect on corridor costs (P50 before/after) and on the 30-minute test; what could not be done.
- Handoff (`milestone-handoff`): tests on a fixture corridor (10 km synthetic DEM), `make test`, docver, manifest, `red-team-verifier` in the foreground (label `R4_ALIGN`; 10 random corridors re-routed from the frozen weights must reproduce byte-identically), commits, `R4_ALIGN: done` or `blocked - <reason>`.
