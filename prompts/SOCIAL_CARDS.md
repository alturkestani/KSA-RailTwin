Goal: 5 photo cards for social replies, each in English and Arabic (10 PNGs), 1080x1350, made from the study's own data and maps (same look as the film's sim_map frames, not the report charts). Plain language, no finance jargon (no NPV, BCR, P50/P90, capex), large text readable on a phone, at most about 25 words per card, full tashkeel not needed. Footer on every card: "Independent simulation, not a government plan · github.com/alturkestani/KSA-RailTwin" (Arabic equivalent on AR cards). OSM attribution where a map is shown.

Cards:
1. The connected network map: Tabuk to Jazan, Najran, Abha and Khamis Mushait, with existing, committed and proposed lines clearly told apart.
2. What it would cost to build: the committed lines about 100 billion riyals (likely range about 50 to 470), the full connected network about 230 billion (range about 120 to 860), the three new stations about 1.4 billion. Use the exact values from the report/top5 and the S6-L6 score, rounded, and say the ranges allow for overruns like past rail projects.
3. Riyals back per riyal spent: three stations about 6, Al-Kharj spur about 2.3, committed lines about 0.3, Riyadh–Jeddah about 0.2, connected network about 0.06. Simple horizontal bars with a line at 1 and the label "below 1 = doesn't pay back what it costs".
4. Riyadh–Jeddah: train vs flight door to door, using the film's own figures.
5. How it was made: 94 Claude runs, about USD 1,325 at list price (subscription), about USD 7 in voices, open code. Take the figures from docs/cost-log.md and docs/spend-summary.md at HEAD.

Every number must trace to a file and line in the repo; list those sources in docs/publish/social-cards.md. Arabic wording in plain Saudi Arabic, consistent with the reviewed film.
