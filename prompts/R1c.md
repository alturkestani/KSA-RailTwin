## Tasks
1. **Register schema and CLI** (`cost-register`): pydantic `CostItem` in `common/schemas.py` (additive change) and `costs/register.py` with `validate`, `normalise --to SAR --price-year <y>` (writes `cost_register.normalised.yaml`, never edits the source), `list --category ...`. `cities/ksa/costs/index.yaml` holds every inflation and FX index with its source (e.g. GASTAT CPI, central-bank rates, OECD deflators); a derived item names its index in `derivation`.
2. **KSA data points** (top-down): Haramain HSR (civil works packages, the systems/operation consortium contract, total programme cost as published; ridership vs forecast where any forecast was published), SAR North and East lines, Riyadh Metro (the ENR "USD 25 B" headline verified against RCRC/SPA statements), Landbridge estimates (both the SR 100 bn and USD 7 bn figures with scope, from `docs/sources/conflicts.md` if DATASCOUT_1H wrote it). Each as `status: sourced` rows with low/central/high reflecting the spread of published figures, price year and currency; unknowns written as `unknown` rows (item named, value empty, `notes: not published; searched <where>`).
3. **Bottom-up library** (per km and per item, by type and terrain) from published unit-cost studies and completed projects worldwide (use R1b's `cities/ksa/benchmarks/metrics.csv` and `overruns.csv` if present on `main`; otherwise cite your own): line types HSR ≥ 250 km/h, conventional passenger, mixed freight/passenger, commuter, upgrade of existing; terrain classes flat desert, sand dunes (sand mitigation and maintenance), sabkha, escarpment/mountain (Sarawat/Asir, Hejaz); structures (tunnel and viaduct shares and unit costs); systems (stations by class, depots, rolling stock by type, signalling by ERTMS level, electrification vs diesel/hybrid/hydrogen, power supply); land and utilities; design, project management, contingency. Items with no source anywhere become `assumed` with a range and a one-sentence reason, registered in `docs/assumptions-r1c.md`.
4. **Opex, lifecycle and financing**: train operation, energy, maintenance of way (incl. sand), staffing with a Saudisation-share parameter (assumption), renewals over 30/40/60 years; financing-cost parameters (sovereign borrowing, PIF, project finance, export credit; interest during construction) as sourced or assumed ranges. No value is a single point.
5. **Reference-class uplifts** (`reference-class-forecasting`): read the Green Book supplementary guidance on optimism bias and the published reference-class literature for rail (capex, schedule, ridership); write `cities/ksa/costs/uplifts.yaml` with distributions per line type and stage, each with its source and a registered assumption id; `costs/rcf.py apply` draws capex, schedule and ridership multipliers (seeded) and writes P10/P50/P80/P90 plus the full distribution.
6. **Estimator**: `uv run python -m costs.estimate --corridor tests/fixtures/corridor.yaml --register cities/ksa/costs/cost_register.yaml --uplifts cities/ksa/costs/uplifts.yaml --seed 1 --out runs/costs/fixture/` gives P10/P50/P90 capex and opex for the fixture corridor from terrain shares, structure shares and system choices; `meta.json` via `common.runmeta`. Tests: schema validation, normalisation round trip, determinism, and that every fixture item resolves to a register id.
7. **`docs/costs.md`**: version header; the register summary by category; KSA data points table with sources and the Landbridge conflict as written in conflicts.md; bottom-up vs top-down reconciliation per line type (gap > 30 % explained or flagged); uplift tables with sources; the list of `unknown` and `assumed` items; label "cost ranges, price year <y>, not a budget".
8. **Handoff** (`milestone-handoff`): `make test`, `red-team-verifier` (10 % spot-check of register rows against their URLs), commits per task, status block in `docs/status/r1c.md` (rows sourced/derived/assumed/unknown, KSA data points found, uplift sources, fixture P50 range with run id). End with `R1c: done` or `R1c: blocked - <reason>`.
