## 1. Phantom HHR chord in Jeddah — fix the geometry (Tariq's diagnosis, verified by the coordinator)
Tariq's map is right: the straight line on the west, from Al-Sulaimaniyah to KAIA, does not exist. The line from Al-Sulaimaniyah to the airport is the same Haramain line that comes from Makkah, not a second trunk.

In `cities/ksa/lines/geojson/hhr.geojson` (copied as `existing.geojson` feature 0) the cause is a **LineString stitch**: the feature is one LineString of 1,100 points; after index 376 the coordinates are at KAIA (39.1737763, 21.660417) and index 377 jumps **17.3 km** to Al-Sulaimaniyah (39.2444797, 21.5192451), then traces the main line north again (the Sulaimaniyah–junction stretch is drawn twice; index 320 and index 431 are the same point, 39.215395, 21.6234465). That jump is the false straight chord. The file measures **484.6 km** (haversine over the vertices) against `length_km: 453` in `cities/ksa/lines/hhr.yaml`.

The correct layout, already stated in `hhr.yaml` notes (S-004) and in OSM ("Jeddah Airport branch line"), is: **one main line Makkah – Al-Sulaimaniyah – (junction north of the city) – KAEC – Madinah, plus a short ~3.75 km branch from that junction to KAIA.**
