## Schema and working rules (merged PILOT_MERGE schema, CP1_PREP 31 Oct 2026)
- Field names are exactly those of `common/schemas.py:RegionRow` (table in the `region-sweep` skill): `osm_id` **and** `osm_type` (node/way/relation; ids overlap across types), `dist_existing_km`, `nearest_existing`, `dist_committed_km`, `nearest_committed`, `nearest_committed_station`, `dist_metro_km`, `nearest_metro`, `nearest_metro_tier` (1/2/3, A-screen-013), `road_min_*`, `road_min_method`, `dist_method`, `tag_10k`, `tag_5k` (never `threshold_5k`), `notes`.
- Population provenance: `pop_type` is `official` / `derived` / `grid` only. A GASTAT figure relayed by an aggregator (e.g. citypopulation.de) is `pop_type: official` + `pop_source_status: secondary` + `primary_to_find: <the GASTAT table>`; a figure read on GASTAT itself is `pop_source_status: verified`. Never `pop_type: secondary`. Use the settlement (place/locality) figure, never the governorate total, for a town row.
- **Population rule (Tariq, CP2, 31 Oct 2026; A-screen-031…033), in his words:** "GASTAT-relayed figures win where they exist; WorldPop fills gaps only, every gap row flagged `pop_type: grid`, `pop_source_status: unverified`. A grid-only settlement may pass the ≥ 10k test only if a second independent method (building footprints × occupancy, GHSL, or VIIRS-calibrated) corroborates it within 30 %." So: a `grid` row gets `tag_10k: true` only with `pop_corroboration` (`buildings_x_occupancy` / `ghs_pop_sum` / `viirs_calibrated` / `gastat2010_city_growth`) and `pop_corroboration_value` within abs(grid − value) / grid ≤ 0.30; otherwise `tag_10k: false` and `exclusion_reason: "grid-only population, uncorroborated"` (keep `screened: true`; `tag_5k` is not gated). Leave both corroboration fields null unless a second method was actually run (the 2010-growth value is in `runs/gastat/apply/cities2010_growth.csv` for matched towns); never put a corrected or derived estimate into `population`. The grid sensitivity is the funnel's job (`screening.funnel sensitivity-grid`), not yours.
- **GASTAT addenda (Tariq, 31 Oct 2026):** the **primary** Census 2022 TA1 exports `data/ksa/tables/gastat/census2022_TA1_governorate_primary.xlsx` (150 governorates, S-cp2-gastat-007) and `census2022_TA1_city5k_primary.xlsx` (271 cities of 5,000 or more plus "Other (< 5,000)" = 3,683,816, S-cp2-gastat-008) "supersede the relayed file at governorate and city level" (addendum 2, 15:25): a settlement matched to a primary city gets that figure as `pop_type: official`, `pop_source_status: verified`, and "the ≥ 10k test for those rows uses the primary figure only (no corroboration needed)" (matching tiers and guards A-screen-038; crosswalk `screening/gastat_crosswalk.yaml`); the DataPulse relayed table (S-cp2-gastat-001) is a cross-check only, "never the value of record where a primary row exists"; keep citypopulation.de only for localities neither table covers; log a conflict where figures differ by more than 2 %. Grid-filled populations outside the matched cities are checked against each region's residual (control total A-screen-037; `control_total_flag`, reporting only). Below city level keep the WorldPop gap-fill under the gate, with "a third method: the 2010 cities > 5k list grown at the governorate's 2010→2022 ratio" (`pop_corroboration: gastat2010_city_growth`, value = pop_2010 × gov_2022 / gov_2010, A-screen-035), which "counts as one of the independent corroboration methods". The sensitivity uses the back-test median against the primary cities: ÷ 0.71 (A-screen-039; earlier ÷ 0.48, A-screen-036, and ÷ 0.64, A-screen-034). Lookups and the 2010 estimates: `screening.gastat` (`tables`, `apply`, then `primary`; `runs/gastat/apply/cities2010_growth.csv`).
- `terrain_class` uses the schema words only: `flat_desert`, `dunes`, `sabkha`, `escarpment_mountain`, `coastal`, `mixed` (not `sand_dunes` or `coastal_plain`).
- `economic_roles`: list of `{role, source}` with role in `port, industrial_city, mining, tourism, heritage, hajj_umrah, airport, university, giga_project, agriculture, border_crossing, military, administrative, energy` (`energy` = refineries, oil/gas processing, export terminals). Every role names an `S-` source that ties it to that town; an empty list means "none sourced", say in `notes` if roles were not assessed.
- `threshold_50k` and `pilot_sample` are pilot-only columns: leave them null in the R2 register.
- Assumption ids are prefixed by your slug (`A-tabuk-001`, ...), never the shared `A-screen-pilot-00n`.
- **Scripts and raw files:** every builder or helper script lives in the worktree under `screening/sweeps/tabuk/` and is committed; never write scripts to `/tmp`. Raw downloads (Nominatim/Overpass JSON, census tables) go under `data/ksa/tabuk/raw/` (gitignored) and get a row in `cities/ksa/sources.yaml` (`uv run python -m common.manifest add ...`) before anything is computed from them.
- **Status line:** the last line of your status block is plain text at the start of the line, with no backticks, bold, bullet or indentation: `REGION_TABUK: done` or `REGION_TABUK: blocked - <reason>`.
