2. **Fiscal exposure** (`fiscal.py`): for each programme and model, the state's direct capex share, availability payments as a share of a published budget reference (sourced; else registered assumption with range), contingent liabilities (demand guarantees, termination payments) sized from the R5 draws (P10/P50/P90), and the timing profile over the horizon. `docs/fiscal-exposure.md` with tables per programme and envelope, labelled as scenario comparison.

Options are presented side by side; the recommendation is argued from the evidence and marked as the analyst's judgement.
5. **Tests**: risk matrix validates and covers every row/column; fiscal exposure arithmetic on the fixture CBA case; VfM switching value solver.
6. **Handoff** (`milestone-handoff`): `make test`, `red-team-verifier` (advocacy check, sources, ranges, unclear-law flags), commits per task, status block in `docs/status/r7.md` (families covered, models recommended, fiscal exposure ranges with run ids, unclear items count).
