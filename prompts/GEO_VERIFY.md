# GEO_VERIFY — geospatial verification and estimation (track r1a, after R1a; claude-opus-5-5)

Tariq's standing method (31 Oct 2026) is in `.claude/skills/geo-verify/SKILL.md`; read it first and follow it literally. You run in the `r1a` worktree after the `R1a` label has written `cities/ksa/lines/` and the skims; use them. **Time-box: 75 minutes of wall time** (the coordinator budgets USD 40 list for this label and checks the cost log). At 60 minutes stop starting new work, finish the method log and the status block.

## You own
`screening/geo_verify/` (new package, CLI, tests), `docs/geo-verify.md`, `cities/ksa/lines/stations.geojson` (adds and `location_method` fields only), `cities/ksa/regions/pilot-*.parquet|csv` (derived populations and flags only; never an official figure), `cities/ksa/candidates-pilot.parquet` (re-merge), `data/ksa/geo/` (gitignored cache ≤ 5 GB), `docs/sources/conflicts.md` (your rows), `docs/assumptions-r1a.md` (`A-geo-` rows), `docs/licences-r1a.{md,csv}` (imagery/grid/footprint rows), `cities/ksa/sources.yaml` (manifest rows for every file you download), `docs/status/r1a.md` (your block), `.claude/agents/rail-data-scout.md` and `.claude/skills/region-sweep/SKILL.md` (one paragraph each: "use geo-verify for every unlocated or unsourced row").

## Tasks (priority order from the skill §2; stop where the time-box ends)
1. **Package and gates first** (≤ 20 min): `screening/geo_verify/` with `cli.py` (`stations`, `seat`, `estimate`, `backtest`, `validate-geocodes`, `served-rerun`; `--seed`), `geom.py` (UTM zone by longitude, geodesic distance), `imagery.py` (rasterio windowed COG reads; cache under `data/ksa/geo/` with a size check and the 15 GiB free stop), `extents.py` (DBSCAN on footprint centroids; morphological close on GHS-BUILT-S), `estimators.py`, `method_log.py`. Tests on small fixtures. Register the data sources you will touch in the licence register **before** downloading (Sentinel-2 via the Copernicus Data Space STAC or AWS open bucket; GHS-BUILT-S and GHS-POP R2023A tiles; WorldPop 2020 constrained KSA; Microsoft footprints for Saudi Arabia; VIIRS annual composite) with licence, URL, access date, and in the manifest with SHA-256 after download.
2. **Back-test gate** (≤ 10 min): choose 10 settlements with Census 2022 populations in the register (stratified: 2 above 500k, 3 of 50–500k, 3 of 10–50k, 2 below 10k); run both estimators; write `docs/geo-verify.md` §back-test with the median absolute % error per method; decide (and state) whether each method may rank.
3. **Use case a — stations** (≤ 15 min): Al-Sulaymaniyah HHR (Jeddah) first; then any station or Landbridge stop still unlocated in `cities/ksa/lines/`; imagery confirmation window saved as a method-log entry (bbox, date of scene, what was seen), **no imagery crops committed**. Rerun the served test and write the before/after served list.
4. **Use case b — Al-Qarā and Al-Khamasin** (≤ 10 min): footprint from built-up clusters; point-in-polygon and km-to-boundary against the Al-Baha governorate polygon for the Wikidata point; seat-town identification for Wadi ad-Dawasir; conflict rows.
5. **Use case c — estimates** (remaining time): every register row with no population or only a secondary one, two methods each, central + band, `pop_type: derived|grid`, `pop_source_status: secondary`, `primary_to_find`, method-log id in `notes`; > 25 % divergence from an official or relayed figure → conflict row; never overwrite.
6. **Use case d — Qassim geocodes** (≤ 5 min): point-in-polygon validation, manifest rows, flags.
7. Handoff (`milestone-handoff`): `make test`, docver, manifest verify, status block with: what ran, back-test errors, stations located (count, names), rows estimated (count, how many may rank), conflicts logged, cache size, disk free, minutes used; then `GEO_VERIFY: done` or `GEO_VERIFY: blocked - <reason>`. The `red-team-verifier` spot-checks 10 derived values against the imagery windows and the method log (VERIFY_R1 covers it; you may also run it yourself if time remains).

## Rules that bite
No Google-derived data of any kind in the repo (visual checks are noted as "visual check, Google Maps, <date>" only). Open-first and logged (rule 12). Ranges and bands, never points. Disk: stop below 15 GiB free. No national mosaics. No real-world action.
