## 1. Style

**Picture sources.** Every frame comes from the twin's own registers and `runs/` outputs, from the public-domain archival candidates in `film/rights_log.csv`, or from plain text cards. Nothing is animated by hand and no figure is typed in: chart scenes read the run files named in `data_source` at render time. There are no invented Saudi places, stations, trains, deserts or people. The cold-open journeys use maps, line art (a car glyph, a berth glyph, an air arc) and data cards, never actors, stock footage or generated imagery. No logo or emblem of SAR, PIF, HHR, any ministry or any company appears anywhere. AI-generated footage: none planned (`ai_generated: false` on every shot).

**Fonts** (SIL OFL 1.1; licence pages read today; rows in `film/rights_log.csv` and `film/licences-r10.csv`):
- Arabic captions, lower thirds and footers: **Noto Naskh Arabic** (the dashboard's Arabic font, which keeps film and dashboard consistent). Data cards may use **IBM Plex Sans Arabic**. **Amiri** is optional, for title cards only.
- Latin: **Noto Sans** (the dashboard's UI font). **IBM Plex Sans** is the alternative when Plex Sans Arabic is used on the same card.
- Normal tracking everywhere (no letter-spacing). Arabic is shaped and RTL-correct, checked on a rendered frame with `common.ar_render` bidi-probe (measured, not eyeballed), with digits in Western form as in the script.

**Palette and layers** (colour-blind-safe pairs, and identical to the dashboard, `web/src/config.ts`, rule 5):

| Layer | Colour | Line style | Legend text EN / AR |
|---|---|---|---|
| existing | #1F4E79 (dark blue) | solid | existing / قائم |
| committed / under procurement | #D97706 (amber) | long dash | committed or under procurement / ملتزم به أو قيد الطرح |
| hypothetical (ours) | #7C3AED (violet) | dotted | hypothetical (ours) / افتراضي (من الدراسة) |
| historical | grey (120,120,120) | sparse dots, thin | historical / تاريخي |

Annotation colours never reuse layer colours: the air arc is a thin neutral grey-pink line labelled "air", reach shading is a neutral teal ramp, and highlights are white outlines. Layers differ by dash pattern as well as hue, so the map still reads in greyscale. A **three-layer legend is on screen in every map frame that shows lines** (`layer_legend: true`). Planned lines never look existing.

**Credits on the frame**
- OSM: «© OpenStreetMap contributors», bottom right, on every map frame (`osm_credit: true` on every map, split-screen and archival-with-inset shot).
- Hillshade shots (sh-01-04, sh-06-01, sh-06-03, sh-06-51, and the shots that hold or re-cut the sh-01-04 hillshade: sh-01-05, sh-01-07, sh-01-51) add: "produced using Copernicus WorldDEM-30 © DLR e.V. 2010-2014 and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014-2018 provided under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA; all rights reserved". The credits roll carries the no-liability notice.
- Basemap: the plan is `?basemap=none` (plain background). If OpenFreeMap Positron is used instead, add «© OpenMapTiles».

**Footers** (bottom left, two lines, Noto Sans / Noto Naskh Arabic, on every frame of the shot):
