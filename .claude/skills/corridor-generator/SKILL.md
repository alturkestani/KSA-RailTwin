# Corridor-family generation

Input: `cities/ksa/candidates.parquet` rows with `funnel_stage = short`, plus the W1 layers `cities/ksa/lines/existing.geojson` and `committed.geojson`. Output: `cities/ksa/corridors/<family>/<id>.yaml` (line YAML, `layer: hypothetical`) and `scenarios/S2.yaml` … `S5.yaml` (`scenario-yaml`).

Command: `uv run python -m screening.corridors generate --candidates cities/ksa/candidates.parquet --lines cities/ksa/lines --rules cities/ksa/assumptions/corridor_rules.yaml --seed 1 --out cities/ksa/corridors`

## Families
| family | scenario | generated how |
|---|---|---|
| trunk gaps | S2 | pairs of metro areas or regional centres (short list, population above A-corr-001) with no existing or committed line between them; alignment = shortest road-graph path on the OSM graph with terrain cost (A-corr-002); e.g. Jeddah–Riyadh HSR vs the S1 mixed Landbridge |
| branches and spurs | S3 | each short-list town with an economic role and > A-corr-003 km from a line: spur from the nearest existing or committed line node; group towns within A-corr-004 km into one branch |
| commuter belts | S4 | for each metro (Riyadh, Jeddah, Makkah, Madinah, Dammam): every short-list town 40–120 km by road (A-screen-005), a radial or chord line serving the towns on each sector |
| station additions | S5 | short-list towns within A-corr-005 km of an existing or committed alignment that have no station: a new station with the through-passenger time penalty from `pareto-optimizer` |

Every rule distance is an assumption in `corridor_rules.yaml` with `assumption:` text and a range; register it.

## Each corridor YAML (extends `line-yaml`)
```yaml
id: S3-asir-abha-khamis-spur
family: branch_spur
layer: hypothetical
source: generated by screening.corridors from candidates.parquet <sha256> with corridor_rules.yaml <sha256>, seed 1
generated_from: [candidate ids]
alignment: cities/ksa/corridors/branch_spur/S3-asir-abha-khamis-spur.geojson   # OSM-graph path, ODbL credit
length_km: 118.4
terrain_shares: {flat_desert: 0.3, escarpment_mountain: 0.7}   # from DEM, source S-xxx
structures_share: {tunnel: 0.12, viaduct: 0.08}                 # from terrain rule A-corr-006, assumed
technology_options: [conventional_passenger, mixed_freight_passenger]
stations: [...]                                                 # from line-yaml
connects_to: {line: existing-sar-north, node: ...}
```

## Rules that bite
- Nothing hard-coded: a family must be reproducible from the register + rules + seed; the verifier re-runs the command and diffs.
- Planned lines (Landbridge, GCC, Riyadh Rail Link) stay `committed`; a hypothetical corridor that duplicates one is reported as "dominated by S1" rather than generated.
- Every corridor needs a cost range (`cost-register`) before it enters a scenario; a corridor without costs is listed as "uncosted" and excluded from CBA.
- Alignments are indicative (graph paths), not engineering alignments; say so in every doc.
- Station siting (A-station-001, adopted by Tariq, 6 Nov 2026; `docs/stations-siting.md`): main stations are not colocated on airport land. `generate` runs `siting_check`, which tests every station it adds (not from `stations.geojson`, not `airport` class) against the airport points at 1.5 km: a station addition (S5) on airport land is refused, and a station in any other family is flagged in `siting_flags` (not moved). Both are recorded as decision rows. An airport stop is an extra halt on a short branch, never a substitute for the city station.
