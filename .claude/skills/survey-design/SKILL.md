# Survey design (design only)

Rule 7: surveys are designed only; humans field them. Claude never contacts respondents, never posts a form, never creates an online survey account. Outputs live in `docs/survey/`.

## Files
`docs/survey/design.md` (purpose, hypotheses, attributes, levels, design method, sample), `docs/survey/instrument.en.md`, `docs/survey/instrument.ar.md` (written natively in Arabic, MSA with a Saudi-friendly register; not machine-translated), `docs/survey/ethics-consent.md`, `docs/survey/analysis-plan.md`, `docs/survey/choice_sets.csv` (the generated choice tasks), `cities/ksa/assumptions/mode_choice_priors.yaml` (literature priors, each `source:`).

## Stated-preference / discrete-choice design
- Choice context: intercity trip between a named origin and destination pair drawn from the respondent's region (shown as a scenario, not a hypothetical line name).
- Alternatives: car (own), rail (new service), air (where a pair has flights), bus/coach.
- Attributes and levels (each level sourced to a plausible range from the cost register and timetable model): in-vehicle time (3 levels), access/egress time (3), fare in SAR (3–4), frequency/headway (3), reliability (on-time share, 2), comfort class (2), women-only or family carriage available (yes/no), prayer facilities on board or at stations (yes/no), luggage allowance (2), first/last-mile option in summer heat (shaded/air-conditioned transfer yes/no).
- Design: D-efficient fractional factorial with priors from the literature; 8–12 choice tasks per respondent; blocks; one dominance check task; generated by `uv run python -m demand.survey design --config docs/survey/design.yaml --seed 1 --out docs/survey/choice_sets.csv` (use an open package, e.g. `pyDOE3`/own code; register it).
- Also collect: revealed trips in the last 12 months by mode and purpose (commute, business, VFR, pilgrimage, tourism), household car availability, licence, employment, remote-work days, income band (optional), gender, age band, nationality (Saudi/non-Saudi), region and governorate of residence.

## Sample design
Stratified by region (13) × urban/non-urban × gender, with minimum cells; target n per region computed from the expected coefficient precision (write the formula and the assumption id); oversample small towns on the short list. Mode of fielding recommended (online panel, intercept at stations/airports, household) with cost ranges sourced and marked as estimates.

## Ethics and consent
Purpose statement; voluntary participation; anonymity; data minimisation (no names, no national ids, no exact addresses); storage and retention; PDPL (Saudi Personal Data Protection Law) considerations cited; contact for questions to be filled by the human fielding team; a clear statement that the study is independent and non-partisan.

## Analysis plan
Multinomial and mixed logit estimation plan (open software: Biogeme, `xlogit`, or PyMC; register it); value-of-time derivation by purpose and income band; willingness to pay for women-only carriages, prayer facilities and reliability; checks (dominance task failures, protest responses); how estimates replace the literature priors in `mode_choice_priors.yaml` (status `assumed` → `calibrated`).

## Rules that bite
- Every coefficient used in the model carries `status: literature-based until survey results exist` in `docs/assumptions.md`.
- The Arabic instrument is reviewed by a native speaker before any fielding (recorded as a checkpoint item for Tariq).
- No fictitious survey results, ever; the pilot and reports say "designed, not fielded".
